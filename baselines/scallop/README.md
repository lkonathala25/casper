## Warcraft (`warcraft/`)

Ported from `/home/lk1325/CTSketch/warcraft_12x12_baseline/`: `scallop_warcraft.py`
(trainer), `decode_map_warcraft.py` (argmax vs top-1 proof / Dijkstra decode),
`warcraft_path.scl` (training program) and `warcraft_reach.scl` (decode program).

**Adapted:** dataset, backbone, loss, per-cell categories and metrics now come from
`tasks/warcraft/` -- the modules CASPER uses -- instead of the upstream forks
`train_unconstrained.py`, `warcraft_node_categories.py` and `compute_extra_metrics.py`.

- `--alphabet full` is the default (868 outputs at 12x12). The upstream fork had no
  alphabet option and was monotone-only (628), and its label converter silently
  relabelled cells on non-monotone paths as `skip`. The shared converter raises.
- The backbone is `tasks/warcraft/model.ResNet18NodeCategorical` with
  `constraint_mode='none'`: CASPER's own ResNet-18 and head class.
- Data resolves to `data/warcraft/12x12/preprocessed_4conn` (maps and labels
  md5-identical to upstream).
- `--train-limit N` added for smoke tests.

The loss is unchanged: per-cell CE + `lambda * -log P(valid_path)`, lambda = 0.1,
k = 3, `difftopkproofs`. The shared CE is numerically identical to upstream's (both
average over all image x cell pairs).

**Constraint: steps encoding (`--constraint steps`, the default).** The training
program is generated by `steps_program()` from the same compiled steps artifact
CASPER loads (`constraints/warcraft/steps_artifacts/steps_12_trimmed_compiled.json`):
`at(i, v)` is the walk X_0..X_L over the compiled domains D_i, each move follows a
probabilistic edge fact (the per-cell categories averaged over the edge's two
endpoints, as before), the sink absorbs, and the query is X_L = sink. With exact
ground-truth edges it gives P(valid) = 1 on all 57 non-monotone and 7 monotone test
boards checked, and 0 once any path edge is removed. `--constraint reach` keeps the
original transitive-closure `warcraft_path.scl`.

Cost: about 2.8x the reach program per training step (42 s vs 15 s for 256 boards)
and 8.7 GB vs 5.1 GB peak RAM.

Still to note: the loss scores walks, so it does not by itself forbid extra edges
off the walk (same as the reach program); and `decode_map_warcraft.py` still decodes
with `warcraft_reach.scl`.

**Run:** `run_warcraft.sh` -- 3 seeds x 50 epochs, each followed by the MAP decode.
